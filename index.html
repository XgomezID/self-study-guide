Online Time Management Strategy Guide

Weekly Self-Regulation Planner

In this module, you learned about tips and strategies to succeed in an online program, from managing your time and study habits, to engaging thoughtfully in discussions, to navigating group work, all while balancing the competing obligations and life experiences you bring into your coursework. Those strategies work best when they're not just a checklist you follow once, but a habit you build and adjust over time. That's where this worksheet comes in.

It's built around a well-known framework in educational psychology called self-regulated learning, developed by researcher Barry Zimmerman. Learning isn't just something that happens to you during a lecture or a reading. It's something you actively manage, especially when you're studying on your own schedule. Zimmerman's model breaks that management process into three phases that repeat in a loop, week after week.

1. Forethought Before you start: set a goal and plan your approach.
2. Performance While you work: monitor focus and adjust as needed.
3. Self-Reflection After you finish: evaluate, and carry it forward.
